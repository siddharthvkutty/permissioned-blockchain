Validator admission

Being a member of the network (having a wallet) and being authorized to mine are two separate things here. Only addresses approved below can request or redeem a mining certificate - everyone else's requests are rejected by this service, even if their node is fully synced and their proof-of-work is valid. This whitelist lives only on the MCA, never inside node-replicated data, so no node operator can grant themselves mining rights by editing their own local files.

To find someone's address, have them check their Wallet dashboard - it's shown right under their balance.
